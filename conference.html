Conference 2027: Powering the Future

January 22, 2027 · 9am–5pm · Gleacher Center, Downtown Chicago

For the first time in decades, US energy demand is growing in a meaningful way. AI, data centers, and widespread electrification are all driving a surge in electricity demand, and with it, rising costs and new pressure on the grid.

How do we meet this moment and power the future in a way that is affordable, reliable, and sustainable?

Our annual conference will explore the technology, finance, and policies shaping the future of energy. Hear from regulators, utilities, energy developers, investors, and founders on the capacity challenge, the cost of interconnection, and the capital needed to finance the future.

Speakers

Panel · 9:40am–10:30am

Plugging In: Policy, Queues, and the Cost of Connection

Doug P. Scott

Chairman, Illinois Commerce Commission

Doug was appointed Chairman of the Illinois Commerce Commission by Gov. Pritzker in 2023. Doug previously served as Chairman of the ICC 2011–2015, helping Illinois expand its renewable energy usage while keeping consumer utility bills affordable. Previously, Doug was Energy Systems VP at the Great Plains Institute, and spent 25 years in public sector positions. Doug received his B.A. from the University of Tulsa and a J.D. from Marquette University.

Nicole Luckey

Senior Vice President, Regulatory Affairs, Invenergy

Nicole is the SVP of Regulatory Affairs at Invenergy, a leading owner-operator of energy assets. She leads teams engaged in RTO/ISO stakeholder processes impacting interconnection, development, and operation of generation assets and implements regulatory advocacy strategies throughout the organization. Prior to Invenergy, she was the Legal & Policy Advisor to Chairman Doug Scott and Staff Attorney at the ICC. Nicole is a graduate of the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and the Chicago-Kent College of Law.

Kristin Munsch

Vice President, Regulatory Policy and Strategy, Commonwealth Edison

Kristin is VP for Regulatory Policy and Strategy for ComEd, an electric utility serving 4 million customers. Her work focuses on regulatory policy, including the implementation of Illinois' Climate & Equitable Jobs Act. Prior to this position, she served as Senior Associate General Counsel for ComEd. Previously, Kristin was the Director for Regulatory and Customer Strategy at National Grid and the Deputy Director at the Illinois Citizens Utility Board. Kristin is a graduate of Northwestern and the Chicago-Kent College of Law.

Brad Klein (Moderator)

Managing Attorney, Environmental Law & Policy Center

Brad is a Managing Attorney at the Environmental Law & Policy Center, where he works to accelerate renewable energy development, protect clean water, and promote sustainable development. Brad previously served as a federal judicial law clerk in Washington, D.C., a law fellow at the Environmental Law Institute, and an environmental engineer at CH2M Hill. Brad received his law degree with honors from New York University School of Law and a bachelor's degree in chemical engineering from Washington University in St. Louis.

Keynote · 1:30pm–2:20pm

From the Ground Up: Building Mazama Energy and the Future of Geothermal

Sriram Vasantharajan

Chief Executive Officer and President, Mazama Energy

Sriram is President and CEO of Mazama Energy, a company dedicated to advancing technologies and methods to extract heat from superhot rocks to produce utility-scale, carbon-free energy at fossil electricity prices. Before founding Mazama, Sriram enjoyed a 35-year career in the oil and gas industry. He recently served as CEO and President of Blade Energy Partners, a highly regarded consulting firm focused on providing high-end engineering solutions for the energy industry. Sriram earned a PhD in Chemical Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University. He has authored 50+ scientific publications and holds numerous patents.

Sam Ori (Moderator)

Executive Director, Institute for Climate and Sustainable Growth and EPIC

Sam Ori is the Executive Director at the Institute for Climate and Sustainable Growth and the Energy Policy Institute (EPIC) at the University of Chicago. From 2013 to 2015, he served as EVP at Securing America's Future Energy, an organization dedicated to reducing American oil dependence to enhance economic and national security. Prior to joining SAFE, Sam worked in the federal government at the Broadcasting Board of Governors and Department of State, including at the U.S. Embassy in New Delhi, India.
