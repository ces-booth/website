Members

CES was formed by the merger of Booth's Energy Group and Climate & Sustainability Group. It covers both overlapping and distinct areas, including climate, renewable and traditional energy, and broader sustainability topics such as ESG, the circular economy, and sustainable business practices.

The group is led by a team of seven co-chairs who are passionate about energy careers and community at Booth. Contact us to learn more about CES's presence on campus.
