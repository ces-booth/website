Events

Programming that connects Booth students to the people and ideas shaping the energy transition — from a first-week kickoff to an annual conference with alumni and industry leaders.

Kickoff event

A semester-opening event to introduce CES, its mission, and upcoming activities to the Booth community.

Annual conference

Hosting leading panelists, speakers, sponsors, Booth alumni, and industry experts to explore contemporary perspectives on the energy transition.

Speaker series

Regular sessions with industry experts and alumni to discuss trends, innovations, and career advice.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions covering renewable and traditional energy modeling, energy markets, sustainability reporting, ESG investing, climate finance, and recruiting support across energy, climate, and sustainability careers.

Case competitions

Host or participate in competitions focused on solving real-world problems in energy and sustainability.

Career treks

Visits to energy companies, sustainability-focused organizations, and climate tech startups.

Social events

Networking happy hours, coffee chats, and community-building events for CES members.

Industry summits

With support from the Rustandy Center, CES sends representation to key industry summits — such as ClimateCap and UN COP — staying active in the broader community beyond Chicago Booth.

CES

Climate, Energy & Sustainability Group — a student community at Chicago Booth building the next generation of climate and energy leaders.
