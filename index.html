Empowering the next generation of climate and energy leaders

A community of Booth students committed to addressing climate change, shaping the future of energy, and advancing sustainable practices.

Our mission

Climate action is the biggest issue and opportunity of our time.

CES engages broadly across the full value chain and range of generation technology. Through student-led verticals, educational programming, and career development initiatives, CES facilitates deep industry engagement and strengthens a lifelong network committed to environmental stewardship, innovation, and long-term sustainable growth.

What we cover

CES engages broadly across the full value chain and range of generation technology — from traditional power to the industries building what comes next.

  • Power generation — solar, wind, oil & gas, nuclear, geothermal
  • Utilities and transmission
  • Traditional and alternative fuels
  • Energy storage
  • Energy efficiency and demand response
  • Energy finance and trading
  • Climate, energy, and sustainability consulting
  • Climate, energy, and sustainability finance and investing
  • Circular economy and waste management
  • Low-carbon transportation and electric vehicles
  • Carbon markets
  • Green buildings and sustainable real estate
  • Climate technology and innovation
  • Water management and conservation
  • Corporate sustainability
  • Supply chain management

What we're building toward

Career development

  • Resources and support for members pursuing careers in sustainability, energy, and related sectors after graduation and beyond.
  • Career treks to leading companies and organizations in sustainability and energy.

Education and awareness

  • Events, workshops, and speaker sessions that deepen understanding of climate, energy, and sustainability challenges and opportunities.
  • Knowledge sharing on renewable energy, corporate sustainability, climate policy, and emerging technologies.

Community building

  • A collaborative platform for Booth students with shared interests in sustainability and energy.
  • Relationships with like-minded student groups across the University of Chicago and the broader Chicago community.

Industry engagement

  • Strong ties with corporates, nonprofits, and governmental organizations across climate, energy, and sustainability.
  • Networking events, panels, and conferences featuring industry leaders and alumni.
